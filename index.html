colab-drive

colab-drive 是一个仅供开发者本人使用的个人自动化工具：在开发者自己的 Google Colab 运行时中挂载开发者自己的 Google Drive，用于读写个人项目文件（模型、配置与输出）。它不面向公众提供服务，也不处理任何其他用户的数据。

colab-drive is a personal automation tool used only by its developer. It mounts the developer's own Google Drive inside the developer's own Google Colab runtimes to read and write personal project files. It is not offered to the public and does not process data of any other user.
